How scoring works

Each moss cell costs 1. Spore clearings (+) are free ground — route through them when it's cheap. Thorns (!) cost 3. Mycelium portals cost 1 each — take both ends of a pair and they connect underground, even across the map. Seeds are free. Shortest living network wins. Par is the exact optimum, computed by exhaustive search.

Custom: · χ
How scoring works

Pick a colour, then tap a vertex to paint it. Tap again with the same colour to erase. A solution is valid when every vertex is coloured and no edge joins two equal colours. Score = number of distinct colours used. Match the hidden optimum for ★★★. Dailies come from real applications: maps (neighbouring districts differ — the four-colour theorem says no map needs 5), timetables (exams sharing students can't share a slot, laid out in time order), and SAT reductions (a 3-SAT clause compiled into gadgets — colouring the graph solves the formula), plus classic wheels, rings and propagation chains, and Sudoku (every row, column and box is a clique — givens arrive locked, plus a few extra rivalry links).

Properties explained

E edges · χ chromatic number (fewest colours with neighbours differing) · △ triangles (3 mutually linked dots) · Cyc simple cycles of length ≥ 3 (rotations and reversals count once) · Diam longest shortest-path (∞ if disconnected). Yellow means off by exactly one; ↑/↓ means the answer is higher/lower than your guess; ∞ matches only exactly.

Puzzle 04

Treedle

6 tries

Rebuild the hidden tree. Join all eight dots with exactly seven links and no loops.

exact off by one further away
Properties explained

Leaf leaves, i.e. dots with exactly one link · Diam longest shortest-path (∞ if disconnected) · Δ max degree, the busiest dot · W Wiener index, the sum of all pairwise distances (∞ if disconnected) · α independence number, the largest set of dots with no links between them. Yellow means off by exactly one; ↑/↓ means the answer is higher/lower than your guess; ∞ matches only exactly. Note: a tree on 8 dots always has exactly 7 edges, and 7 edges plus connected guarantees a tree.
